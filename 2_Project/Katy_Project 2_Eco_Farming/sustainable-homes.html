Good homes are part of a good neighborhood.

A sustainable home starts with its shape and setting. Windows bring in natural light, shade helps manage summer heat, and insulation keeps indoor temperatures more comfortable through changing seasons. These passive choices can reduce the need for heating, cooling, and artificial lighting.

Solar panels provide renewable electricity, while efficient fixtures and rainwater collection help make careful use of water. Green roofs and small gardens create space for plants close to home, support local biodiversity, and bring neighbors together around shared growing spaces.
