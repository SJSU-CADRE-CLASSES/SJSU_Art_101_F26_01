A city-sized farm, built from small loops.

Instead of fields beyond the city edge, New Eden grows in stacked greenhouse towers, rooftop beds, and shared neighborhood gardens. Each site uses the same simple principle: give the roots water, oxygen, and exactly the nutrients the crop needs.

Water circulates through shallow channels, past the plants' exposed roots. Sensors keep the flow balanced; filters catch debris and the reservoir is replenished only when needed. Short routes between growers and kitchens mean the harvest can be picked at its best.
