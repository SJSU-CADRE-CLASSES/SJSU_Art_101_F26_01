    More than a place to shop.

    The market is where the city’s growers and neighbors meet. Produce travels a short distance from hydroponic towers, rooftop beds, and community gardens to the stalls, so it can be picked ripe and shared fresh.

    Each tent has its own small harvest: crisp greens, bright fruit, fragrant herbs, and starts for a windowsill or garden plot. Neighbors trade recipes, growers share what is in season, and the commons becomes part of the food system too.
