A thriving harvest begins with a living system.

Inside the Bio-dome, crops grow in shared beds enriched with compost made from plant scraps. Rotating crops and planting a variety of vegetables and herbs helps keep the soil productive, supports beneficial insects, and reduces reliance on a single harvest.

Alongside soil beds, the Bio-dome uses hydroponic channels to grow leafy greens. A measured mix of water and nutrients flows past the exposed roots, then returns to a reservoir to be filtered and reused. This soil-free method helps make good use of vertical space, while composting and crop rotation keep the soil gardens healthy too.
