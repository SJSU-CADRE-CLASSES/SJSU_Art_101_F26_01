The Large Footprint of Mining

Mining requires the burning of fossil fuels through
heavy machinery, on-site generators, and explosives.

The materials being mined are used to create many
products and items that people see or use. To make
these products, the burning of fossil fuels is also
involved.

Etc.

The Social Cost of Minerals

Deep community displacement - People are forced
out of their homes and relocated, especially
indigenous people. Almost 54% of minerals are
located near or in indigenous lands which forces the
indigenous to relocate, lose their traditional
livelihoods, and their social structures.

Health hazards - Mines produce toxic waste that can
flow into nearby water sources causing the water to
be undrinkable. It can also cause respiratory
diseases when people near the mines breathe it in,
and can destroy agricultural fields. This leaves the
people vulnerable.

Labor exploitation - Operations in weak governed
countries experience exploitive working conditions,
child labor, and regional violence.

Etc.

Land Pollution

Destruction, deterioration, or contamination of the
earth’s surface

Municipal Waste - garbage, paper, plastics, food
scraps, discarded appliances

Industrial Waste - chemicals, heavy metals,
electronic waste

Agricultural Practice - chemical fertilizers, pesticides
insecticides

Mining and Extraction - Surface mining and resource
extraction

Deforestation - clearing forest and expanding cities

Etc.

Ocean Pollution

Contamination of marine waters by
plastics, chemicals, oil, and other waste

Plastics - they breakdown into harmful
microplastics that contaminate the
waters

Chemicals - Agricultural fertilizers can
flow into coastal waters, creating “dead
zones” and toxic algal blooms.

Oil and Drilling - poison ecosystems and
is a major problem that leads marine life
towards extinction.

Etc.

Air Pollution

Contamination of environments that alter
the natural composition of the
atmosphere.

Fossil Fuels - burning coal, oil, and gas for
energy generation and transport

Industrial activities - factories and
manufacturing releases toxic gasses into
the air

Etc.

Climate Change

Long-term shifts in temperature and weather
patterns, driven by human activities such as the
burning of fossil fuels.

Etc.
