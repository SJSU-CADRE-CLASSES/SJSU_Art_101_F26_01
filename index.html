Course description

Focus includes programming interactivity, generative graphics, introductory data visualization, and user interface and user experience strategies. Prerequisite: ART 074, ART 075, ART 101, or instructor consent. Six activity hours.

New and emergent media art is marked by its relationship to developing technology. With each advancement in media and communication technologies, early advocates champion democratized idea exchange and positive social impact. These utopian visions are often quickly subsumed by institutional power and control.

The current paradigm of building and working with computers as a medium is rapidly shifting through agentic coding, vibe coding, and AI-assisted building. We will look at early NetArt from the 1990s to the present, highlighting expressive and critical uses of the web, critical and queer interactivity, and tactical media.

Learning outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

  1. LO1: Develop strategies to create custom software as art.
  2. LO2: Develop criteria for evaluating how to design and produce interactive digital media.
  3. LO3: Identify broad trends in interactive digital media and its aesthetics.
  4. LO4: Plan and practice writing simple programs in several programming languages and authoring systems.
  5. LO5: Build HTML/CSS webpages to document artwork and creative process.
