About us / Gaia-Net

One Network for All.

Gaia-Net is a community-driven space for sharing knowledge and resources in the Post-Storm World.
We connect people through practical guides, local events, and notices that help communities rebuild.

Node System

Gaia-Net operates through a decentralized node system, allowing communities to connect and share information efficiently.

You are currently connected to the West Node Sec 01.

Code of Conduct One

Retain

Our purpose is to make useful information easier to find, save, and pass on. From everyday skills to urgent updates, every contribution can help someone nearby.

Code of Conduct Two

Rebuild

Gaia-Net brings together people who share what they know: neighbors, makers, organizers, and local nodes. The network grows through the knowledge and care of its community.

Code of Conduct Three

Respect

Gaia-Net uses a verification process while remaining a community-driven space. Include sources, dates, and locations when sharing information, and flag details that may have changed.

Code of Conduct Four

Render

Share a useful skill, take part in a local event, or pass an important notice to someone who needs it. Browse the bulletin to see what your community is sharing.
