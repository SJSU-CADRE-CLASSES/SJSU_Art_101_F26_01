Hello Luke Isaiah,

Congratulations on submitting your Cal State Apply application!

You will receive next steps from the campus(es) you applied to. All official communications will be via email, so be sure to regularly check this email address.

Here are some important steps to consider in the meantime:

  1. Check your email Spam and/or Junk folders. On occasion, the first email from a university with valuable login information ends up in one of these folders.
  2. Notify the campus(es) you applied to asap if your email address changes from what you submitted on the application.
  3. Complete your financial aid application if you haven’t already. Visit the CSU Financial Aid & Scholarships page for more information.
  4. Set up your personalized portal for the campus(es) you applied to after you receive instructions via email. Once you receive the email, be sure to complete this as quickly as possible.
  5. Send transcripts to the campus you applied to only when requested. You do not need to send transcripts sooner than is requested.
