Game: Roshackbro
Developer: Cursor Entertainment
Platform: PC
Available on: GameJolt.com, Itch.Io
Rating: NR

Description: Based on Pewdiepie's, "World's most dangerous game on the planet," https://youtu.be/U_yMkqdZxcQ. On a game of rock, paper, scissors, the winner of that game will be able to control the other person's (in-game) computer for 30 seconds. The person will have to complete all the missions, like messing up their twatter account or deleting their Utube channel, to win. The player, who's computer is being controlled, can stop the player from completing their missions by setting off offensive traps. One computer will be safe while the other is destroyed.
