CRUNCHYROLL EXPO 2022 IS OFFICIALLY SOLD OUT!

That's right -- Crunchyroll Expo 2022 is officially SOLD OUT of tickets! We've got 2 days to go until the HYPEST EVENT IN ANIME begins, and we can't wait for you to join us in-person and online for Crunchyroll Expo this August 5th - 7th!

For fans who are joining us in-person, make sure to download our Official CRX App on iOS and Android so you'll always have all the info you need for the action-packed weekend ahead. 

Our SCHEDULE is also listed in the mobile app, and you can always visit our website to view all of the scheduled content for both in-person viewing and through our New Crunchy City TV Livestream!

For fans unable to join us in-person, we invite you to join us virtually through our New Crunchy City TV Livestream and VOD replay to participate in all of the exciting Crunchyroll Expo festivities online! 🧡!

GET FAMILIAR WITH OUR CRX MAP!
Joining us in-person at Crunchyroll Expo? Get familiar with our interactive map, available now!

To enter Crunchyroll Expo, be sure you have your Health Check wristband and a valid badge for the day of the convention! Follow the steps below to obtain both:

Once you arrive at Crunchyroll Expo, proceed to Health Check located inside New Crunchy City Hall & Registration, at the corner of Market Street & Viola Ave. (435 S Market St, San Jose, CA 95113)

Please have your ID and proof of vaccination out and ready to be checked. (To view our full health & safety policy, please click here) Once of our friendly staff verifies your proof of vaccination, you will be given a wristband which can be worn throughout your entire stay at Crunchyroll Expo.

If you need to pick up your badge: follow the signs for pick-up & registration. Follow the signs to exit once you have picked up your badge. Due to being sold out of tickets, we are not selling any badges at the door.

If you already have your badge, or once you have picked up your badge, as long as you have gone through our Health Check and received your wristband, you’re ready to enter Crunchyroll Expo! Head to the front of the convention on San Carlos St. where you’ll be able to enter the event!
