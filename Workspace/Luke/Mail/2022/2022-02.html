Dear Luke Isaiah,

You're registered for the October 1, 2022 SAT®. The total cost of your registration and related services is $76.00.

Be sure to bring your admission ticket with you on test day. You can't be admitted without your acceptable ID and a printed admission ticket that matches the information listed on the test center roster. Visit sat.org/test-day for more information.

Please view and print your admission ticket a few days before test day to make sure it's accurate. You can access your admission ticket online by logging in to your free account at collegeboard.org.

Corrections to typos or errors in your personal information must be made by 8 p.m. ET, five days before test day. If you need to correct your ticket, contact Customer Service at sat@info.collegeboard.org or 866-756-7346 (United States) or +1-212-713-7789 (international). Customer Service hours are 9 a.m.–6 p.m. ET, Monday–Friday.

Check your test center's website for any additional or specific entry requirements it may have. If you're traveling to test, it's your responsibility to know and follow any quarantine requirements or travel restrictions in the location where you're testing. Test centers may require proof that you've followed quarantine requirements and/or travel restrictions at check-in.

Learn more about when scores for this administration will be available.

If you requested that scores be sent to colleges, universities, and scholarship programs when you registered, go to collegeboard.org/mysat to view and manage your score send requests. You have until 11:59 p.m. ET, nine days after the test, to make or change your score send choices. Only your scores from October 1, 2022, will be sent unless you change the default.

Before test day, be sure to start preparing for the test with our free, personalized tools at satpractice.org.
