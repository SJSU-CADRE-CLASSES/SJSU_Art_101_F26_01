Can anyone hear me?

Monday, August 17, 2026, 11:42 PM  |  Posted by Luke

I got the old transmitter working last night. It took three days and two blown fuses. I was able to repair it at some point. I thought I heard someone. It was only interference. I wrote it down anyway.

The city is very quiet after dark. From the roof of the waterworks I can see the old towers blinking through the rain. Nobody lives in most of them now. I still look at every lit window and wonder if someone else is up there, listening to the same weather.

The view from the old avenue. The lights are mostly automatic now.

Why I keep broadcasting

I used to tell myself somebody might answer. I don't believe that now. I keep transmitting because the silence is here whether I send or not. At least the machine makes a sound.

This is the only page I still maintain. I made it myself on a borrowed computer, with a hand-me-down modem. If you're reading this, the signal reached another machine. I can't know if anyone is sitting in front of it.

You can leave a note in the guestbook. It stays on this computer. Nobody else will see it.
