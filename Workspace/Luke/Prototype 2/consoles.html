Consoles I Could Not Leave Behind

A few notes on the machines left in the apartments and shops around Sector 08. Most are dead. I keep the ones that still start.

The one that still turns on

Found in the north arcade / August 17, 1998

The cabinet was on its side when I found it. Dust had worked into every seam, and the glass was cracked across one corner. I thought it was dead until the screen flickered when I touched the switch.

It starts, then stops. Sometimes I get a title screen. Sometimes only a line of light. I have not been able to repair it, and I do not know what game is supposed to be inside.

I leave it plugged in anyway. It is another machine making a little noise in an empty building. For now, that is enough.

There is nothing else here.
