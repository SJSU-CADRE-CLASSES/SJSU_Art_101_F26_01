Big Soda (2023)

2023 was my last year in high school and my transition to college. It was a very exciting and stressful time for me. I started to get the final emails from my previous high school, such as scholarships and aid. However, my work started to show some cracks, and I was starting to receive some low scores. Nonetheless, I ended up in San Jose State University in the Animation and Illustration program. 7,519 emails were received in 2023, like a big soda bottle that could feed Earth.
