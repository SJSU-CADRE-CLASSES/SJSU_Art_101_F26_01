Avatar/Data Self Portrait

This is a directed cord diagram which visualizes data to represent connections, relationships, or flows between different categories or things. The diagram consists of arcs or ribbon-like chords that connect between things, showing the directional flow between connections. The width of these arcs indicate the strength of the connections. Directed chord diagrams are usually used to visualize and understand complex systems and their directional relationships.

In the beginning, I thought of a lot of data to record but eventually thought of daily data recordings. My first thoughts were to record 3 words that described my mood everyday. Then, I liked the idea of recording when I feel the urge to buy something, watch something, crave something, etc., and how I feel about them. But as the days passed by, I realized that it would be easier to just record the main emotions/feelings of my everyday life and what caused them. Additionally, while searching for a diagram, I chose a directed chord diagram because I thought it was beautiful with its overlapping ribbon-like arcs and colors going in many directions.

This directed chord diagram visualizes the emotions I was feeling for 18 days. The diagram connects the emotions/feelings I felt with what made me feel them (Ex. I felt happy because I got boba tea). Each data also contains how strong I felt that emotion/feeling from a scale of 1-10. These emotions were recorded on September 3rd-20th of 2026. With each day, I felt emotions that were caused by desires, thoughts, activities, and basically anything throughout my day. This diagram serves as a visual blueprint of my daily life. It not only shows viewers, but it also shows me how I process my life and handle emotions based on what evoked them. Being aware of your emotions and what caused them allows you to manage them.
